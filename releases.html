First public release.

  • Quick Clean with a reason and a safety rating for every category, and a 30-day safety bin.
  • Deep Clean, Sleep Mode and Browser Cleaner through the TotalClean helper.
  • Storage, Apps (with multi-select uninstall and saving install files to Drive), storage map and chat media.

  • Storage forecast, growing apps, device health and security tips.
  • Encrypted vault with fingerprint unlock, private-file finder with Look inside pictures, and an optional app lock.
  • Home screen widget, quick settings tile and your own choice of Home shortcuts.
