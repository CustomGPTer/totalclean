The short version

TotalClean is free. Use it as you like. It deletes files, so look at what is ticked before you clean, and keep copies of anything you cannot afford to lose.

Using the app

  • The app is free. There is no paid version and nothing to buy in it.
  • You decide what is removed. Junk is ticked for you; your own files are only ever removed when you tick them or when a rule you set covers them.
  • Your own files go to the safety bin for 30 days. Junk, and anything you choose to delete for good, cannot be brought back.
  • The vault is protected by a PIN that cannot be reset by anyone. If you forget it, what is in the vault is lost.
  • Files sent to Google Drive are held under Google's terms, in your own account.
  • Use the app only on phones you own or are allowed to manage.
